Personal Equity Portfolio

Investment research & portfolio management

This portfolio started as a way to invest in my future, and it's grown into a way to practice and sharpen my understanding of finance and portfolio management.

My thesis around wide-moat compounders is simple: invest in high-quality businesses with some of the best business models in the world, generally asset-light companies with real, growing cash flows. I believe a concentrated set of those names can outpace the S&P 500 over time through consistent compounding.
